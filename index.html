Does it fit?

A cabin door swings out about 28 in (0.7 m). Rule of thumb used here: 5 ft or more between fronts leaves room to walk past an open door.

The equipment

1Big square bales

  • Your plan: 3 × 4 × 8 ft (0.91 × 1.22 × 2.44 m), the standard “3×4” big-baler size.
  • John Deere’s L341 makes them 120 cm wide × 90 cm high, 0.6–3 m long, tied with 6 twines. Modeled with 6 twines and flakes about every 6 in.
  • About 1,076 lb (490 kg) each at typical hay density; straw weighs less. Placing them takes a loader, telehandler or a tractor with a bale spear.

Sources: John Deere L341 · Texas A&M AgriLife, bale weight

2Portable toilets

  • Modeled on a photo of the actual unit: gray cabin, light-gray arched roof with a raised middle, molded hexagon panels, two big vents on each side, door hinged on the left with a round latch.
  • Sized like PolyJohn’s Fleet, the closest match I found: 47 × 47 × 91 in (1.19 × 1.19 × 2.31 m), 60 gal tank, translucent roof, occupancy latch.
  • Standard cabins run a little narrower: Satellite Maxim 3000 44 × 48 × 90 in, PolyJohn PJN3 43.5 × 47 × 91 in.

Sources: PolyJohn Fleet listing · Satellite Maxim 3000 · PolyJohn PJN3 listing

Stainless trough urinal

  • The run along the right-hand bales is 31.5 ft (9.6 m): four standard 2.4 m trough lengths. A 2.4 m trough is rated for about 4 users without dividers.
  • Floor-standing trough reference: 1065 mm tall, 265 mm deep. DELABIE’s 2.4 m trough (2400 × 575 × 275 mm) takes clip-on stainless dividers.
  • Dividers modeled 24 in deep, 12–60 in off the ground, the US building-code size for urinal screens. Ten dividers make 11 spots about 34 in apart.
  • One drain in the middle, as drawn, means a custom run sloped to the center. In Mexico it is sold as a mingitorio corrido or múltiple de acero inoxidable.

Sources: Commercial Washrooms trough · DELABIE 130120 · IBC 1210.3.2 urinal partitions · Mexican suppliers (Cosmos)

3Hand-wash stations

  • Modeled on a photo of the actual unit: one sink, a back panel with a soap dispenser and a paper-towel box, and a foot pump at the bottom.
  • It looks like PolyJohn’s Encore single-user sink: 62 H × 18 W × 20 D in, 15.5 gal fresh / 19 gal gray water, 47 lb empty, 177 lb full.
  • One sink each, so the 4 units give 4 places to wash. They stand 1 ft off the bales, facing out.

Sources: PolyJohn Encore

What the sketch doesn’t say

  • Scale comes from the bales: four across the back make it 32 ft wide.
  • The toilets are drawn about half their real size, so the middle bale wall is placed to give both aisles the same room (15.5 ft from the left edge when on edge).
  • Toilet spacing along each wall follows the sketch. Doors face the aisles; hinge side, colors and where the urinal drains to are not specified.
